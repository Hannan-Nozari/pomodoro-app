# Privacy Policy — Pomodoro Timer

_Last updated: 14 October 2026_

Pomodoro Timer does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

## What the app stores

The app stores the following on your device only, using your browser's local
storage inside the app:

- your session, break and long-break lengths
- whether the end-of-session sound is on
- your task list
- a rolling history of up to 50 completed focus sessions
- the state of a session that is currently running, so it can be resumed

This information never leaves your device. It is not uploaded, backed up to any
service we control, or shared with anyone. Uninstalling the app deletes it, and
"Clear all" in the session history clears that history immediately.

## What the app does not do

- No accounts and no sign-in
- No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or advertising
- No trackers or third-party SDKs that collect data
- No network requests — the app works fully offline

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Notifications | To alert you when a focus session or break ends |
| Exact alarm | So that alert arrives at the moment your session ends, not minutes later |
| Run at startup | To restore a pending session alert after the device reboots |
| Wake lock | To let the alert be delivered while the device is idle |
| Internet | Required by the Android WebView to load the app's own bundled files; the app makes no network calls |

## Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including
children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published here with a new
